# Strategic SEO Baseline — Outright

**Site:** https://weareoutright.com
**Audit date:** 2026-03-20

---

## Executive Summary

- The site ranks for only **24 keywords** in the US with a total estimated traffic value of **$207/month** — extremely low for an established creative agency
- The dominant revenue driver for organic traffic is the branded term "outright" (sv 5,400, rank 6), which is also a Portuguese-language word and largely navigational
- Key commercial keywords ("creative agency dc", "web design dc") rank in the **bottom half of page 1 or page 2** — not capturing meaningful click-through
- **10 previously ranked keywords have been lost** — the site's organic visibility is actively declining
- The backlink profile has a **spam campaign underway**: 135+ backlinks from 121 domains using a spam anchor text. These are nofollow and low risk but warrant monitoring
- **Zero presence in AI/LLM responses** (ChatGPT) — the site is not cited for any relevant queries
- DC agency competitors have **10–33× more ranked keywords** and estimated traffic values far exceeding Outright's

### Inflated/Low-Value Link Sources

| Source | Backlinks | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| katebatemandesign.com | 185 | Sitewide footer credit ("Agency") across 182 pages — 1 domain, over-counted |

The 185 footer links from katebatemandesign.com represent a single designer's site — not 185 independent endorsements. This inflates the raw backlink count significantly. The actual quality link count is closer to 50–75 unique linking root domains.

The legitimate anchor text is underweight on keyword-rich phrases ("creative agency", "dc branding agency") and overweight on generic or bare-URL anchors.

## 4. AI / LLM Mentions

**ChatGPT:** No appearances found in the DataForSEO LLM mentions dataset.

Outright is not being cited in AI responses for any relevant queries. This is a significant gap as AI Overviews and ChatGPT responses increasingly influence B2B service discovery. The most likely reasons:

- The site has minimal long-form content — LLMs cite sources with substantive, quotable content
- No thought leadership, case study depth, or resource pages that AI engines draw from
- Low domain authority relative to agency directory sites (clutch.co, designrush.com) that do appear for agency search queries

Even fathomcreative.com — a smaller DC shop — has 3× Outright's keyword footprint. Designindc.com has 33× more ranked keywords and appears to have a significantly stronger content and on-page strategy.

Key keywords these competitors rank for that Outright does not (or ranks below page 2):

- "dc branding agency" / "branding agency dc" (~260 sv)
- "nonprofit marketing agency" / "nonprofit branding agency" (~170–260 sv)
- "social impact agency" / "mission-driven agency" (emerging queries)
- "digital agency washington dc" (~320 sv)
- Various "case study" and "portfolio" long-tail terms

Outright's "design for good" positioning should be an advantage for the mission-driven/nonprofit agency keywords, but those terms have no corresponding content on the site.
