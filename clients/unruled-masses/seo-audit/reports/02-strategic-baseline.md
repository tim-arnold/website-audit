# Strategic SEO Baseline — Unruled Masses

**Site:** https://unruledmasses.org
**Audit date:** 2026-03-21
**Domain age:** ~54 days (launched January 26, 2026)

---

## Executive Summary

- The domain is too new to have established keyword rankings or a backlink profile measurable by third-party tools — this report establishes the Day 0 baseline from which all future progress will be measured.
- The site's topical focus (civic engagement, democracy, corruption accountability, nonviolent action) is a competitive content space with established players (Common Cause, Represent.Us, Indivisible, ProPublica). Differentiation will need to come from the Civic Intelligence Platform as a unique data asset.
- No AI/LLM mentions detected in ChatGPT-indexed content. The organization is not yet appearing in AI responses to relevant civic queries.
- The structural content pieces needed for SEO traction (deep playbook content, the intelligence platform, news coverage) are either broken (404), incomplete, or not yet built. Fixing these is the prerequisite for any organic growth.
- The press release published at launch is the only substantive article on the site. A consistent content program is the single highest-leverage SEO investment available.
