## 5. Key Observations

1. **Person pages are the traffic engine.** Most high-volume keywords point to `/person/` URLs. These are the highest-priority pages to preserve.
2. **The .edu backlink profile is exceptionally strong** (113 links). Mostly pointing to Scholars and Emerge program pages. These links took years to build and cannot be replaced.
3. **Old domain redirect chain is load-bearing.** `noblereachfoundation.org` → `noblereach.org` carries 55+ backlinks and many anchor texts still reference the old domain. This redirect must persist through the rebuild.
4. **17 pages are already broken** and have 28 broken backlinks pointing to them. The redesign is an opportunity to fix these.
5. **AI Overview presence is meaningful** but concentrated on a few pages — the innovations article, Dive Technologies Q&A, and person bios.
6. **HubSpot UTM pollution** — at least one ranking URL includes tracking parameters, suggesting the site may be leaking link equity to parameterized URLs.
